# 🎙️ transcribe - Turn spoken words into clear text

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-transcribe-blue.svg)](https://github.com/rithu8989/transcribe/releases)

Transcribe turns audio and video files into text. It uses artificial intelligence to listen to your files and write down exactly what you hear. It runs entirely on your computer. Your files never leave your machine. You keep your data private.

## 🛠️ How it works

The app processes audio locally. It does not send your data to a server. You get results fast. You do not need an internet connection to use it. The app handles various formats, including common audio recordings and video clips. It gives you word-level accuracy. You spend less time editing transcripts.

## 🚀 Using the software

The app interface stays simple. You only need a few clicks to start a transcription.

1. Open the app.
2. Click the "Add File" button.
3. Select the audio or video file you want to transcribe from your computer.
4. Choose the language spoken in the file if the app asks.
5. Click the "Start" button.
6. Wait for the progress bar to reach the end.
7. Review your transcript in the text box.
8. Click "Export" to save your transcript as a text file.

Yes. The app handles long audio and video files. Keep in mind that longer files take more time to process.
